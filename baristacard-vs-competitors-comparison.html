The Details

How each one actually stacks up

A closer look at what you're trading off with each platform.

Loopy Loyalty
£15–25/month

Loopy Loyalty is a straightforward Apple/Google Wallet stamp card tool and does that job well. But it's stamp cards only — no prepaid product cards, no subscription billing, no AI insights — and the fee applies from your very first customer. For a single-location café that just wants a basic punch card, it's a reasonable pick; it just costs more than BaristaCard for less functionality.

Stamp Me
~£39/month

Stamp Me supports a broader range of reward types than Loopy, including points and coupons, and works across more industries than just coffee shops. That flexibility comes at roughly 2.5× the cost of Loopy Loyalty and far more than BaristaCard's free tier — and it still has no prepaid product cards or subscription plans, which are the two features café owners tell us drive the most repeat revenue.

Square Loyalty
£49/month

If you're already running your till on Square, Square Loyalty plugs in natively and is genuinely convenient. But it's the most expensive option in this comparison, effectively locks you into the Square ecosystem, and — like the others — has no prepaid cards, subscriptions, or AI advisor. For cafés not already on Square hardware, switching POS just to get loyalty stamps is a lot of cost for a feature BaristaCard gives away free.

Perkstar
£15/month

Perkstar is the cheapest paid alternative here, and a fair budget option for simple stamp-card loyalty. Its broadcast messaging is more limited than the others, and — the pattern holds — no prepaid cards or subscriptions. At £15/month it's a fine choice if all you need is basic stamps and nothing more, but it still costs more over a year than BaristaCard's £0.

BaristaCard
Free forever (1 venue)

BaristaCard covers everything the others do — Wallet-based stamp cards, broadcast messaging — plus prepaid product cards, monthly subscriptions, digital tipping and an AI advisor that flags at-risk customers and subscription opportunities automatically. Your first venue is free with no time limit and no credit card required; you only start paying if you outgrow one location. We earn a small processing fee on subscriptions and product cards you sell — so we only make money when BaristaCard makes you money.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is BaristaCard really free, or is there a catch?

Your first venue is free forever — no trial period, no credit card required. BaristaCard earns a small processing fee only when you sell subscriptions or prepaid product cards through the platform, so we only make money when you do.

How is BaristaCard different from Loopy Loyalty?

Both offer Apple/Google Wallet stamp cards. BaristaCard additionally includes prepaid product cards, monthly subscriptions, digital tipping and an AI advisor — and is free to start, while Loopy Loyalty charges £15–25/month from day one.

Do I need to switch my point-of-sale system to use BaristaCard?

No. BaristaCard works independently of your POS — customers scan a QR code with their phone camera, no app or hardware change required. This is different from Square Loyalty, which is tied to the Square ecosystem.

Which of these tools is best for a single independent café?

For a single location, BaristaCard's free tier covers the most ground at the lowest cost — unlimited stamp cards, product cards and subscriptions with no monthly fee. If you only need basic stamp cards and don't mind paying, Perkstar (£15/month) is the cheapest paid alternative.
